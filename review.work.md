# Review

Hand review of the philosophy files. Remove a file once reviewed. Remove a task once done.

Order: fewest outgoing links first, mutual pairs adjacent, one cluster at a time.

## How

- Per file, one turn: what changed vs old-claude-reference/rationale/<file>.md first (never the rationale→philosophy move nor the link format), verdict, hazards each with its snippet verbatim, the text verbatim, then wait. Next file only when told.
- A hazard is one line and its snippet verbatim. When one needs a second explanation, the wording is the hazard: say what the words say.
- No skill named or quoted from a philosophy file; an example stays anonymous (sentinel, not NOTHING_TO_REFACTOR).
- Wardley terms as attractors where the path down appears: genesis, custom-built, commodity, commodification. Reference: context-splitting, Where to stop.
- Another session may hold uncommitted edits in philosophy/; stage only the files you changed.
- A file born here has no old counterpart: say where in old-claude-reference its material came from instead.
- Pointer targets are rationales, usually philosophy files: say rationale, not philosophy, where the pointer is meant.
- Pareto attractors (head of the distribution, fat tail) where the usual-case split appears, Pareto named at the first use; never "four in five".
- A named reference (Lakatos, Boyd, Brand) stays one line: it is in the weights.
- abstraction-ladder is the one inheritance case: a sighting says "read it first" and keeps its specific application. Anywhere else a pointer never replaces an attractor: the name stays where its pattern appears.
- What arrives at the engineer is the Observe; the kairos is the moment it opens; metis seizes it. Never "what reaches the human is the kairos".
- Abstraction layering is the pattern; a sighting is a ladder, an instance of it. Outside its own file it is named in full, the abstraction ladder, and "rung" follows that name within the paragraph. Other orderings, inertia or lifecycle, are ladders in their own words.
- Skills and layers 1-2 are self-sufficient projections of philosophy for one case; the executing agent never reads philosophy. They duplicate whatever is relevant: never a hazard.
- Philosophy carries the concept and the essence of a rule; mechanics, forms and exhaustive lists of what not to do are the skill's.
- A file that looks inflated for its topic: list every idea one line each, group by the claim each serves, rewrite from the groups. Deliver the tight text, never a wall with an offer to tighten.
- Reshape in the same turn as the review, and the shape is chosen for the least extraneous load on the reader: every element earns its place by what it saves the reader, each piece in the medium that carries it cheapest (rules/writing.md). The usual outcome: title without "Rationale:", Thesis line, Related, paragraphs that breathe. Backlinks checked for what they promise of the file.
