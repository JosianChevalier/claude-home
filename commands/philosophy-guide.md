Far out in the uncharted backwaters of the unfashionable end of software engineering lies a small unregarded repository.

The Hitchhiker's Guide to Development Philosophy has this to say on the subject of development philosophy: it is big. Really big. You may think your team's coding guidelines are long, but that's just peanuts to this.

The Guide is not definitive, is occasionally inaccurate, and in cases of serious doubt defers to its author, a mostly harmless developer named Josian.

So. Ask it something, or let it take you somewhere. Don't panic: it is mostly harmless either way.
