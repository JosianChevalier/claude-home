## Decided

- The home: `philosophy/mandate.md`, mission command identified in its opening, its parts named only where used.
- The mandate is the perimeter the brief grants, not the brief. It is implicit: a brief that states none grants what was asked, precisely; a sub-agent that finds its change reaching further reports before making it, never comes back with 200 lines.
- What a mandate is: it lists what is in; the exception cannot be listed, enumerating badness (Ranum), so the business as usual is declared and the exception is everything else.
- Where it ends: Taylor's exception principle, overloaded: the standard is an expectation, nobody above watches, the one acting feels the deviation as the task no longer being straightforward: no longer small or fast, an indirection needed, side effects to manage, something else to change first.
- How wide: as far as the brick has proven reliable (mission command's competence and mutual trust); reliable means the result is taken unchecked, no operator over the run.
- The reachability dial, image NASA mission control: the level above engaged in the loop, the off-nominal is reported before acting; the level above away, asleep or in a meeting, it is settled within the intent and named in the debrief. The mecha suit and the autopilot are the two ends.
- What drives the commodification of a brick is the ability to bound its mandate; reliability stays beside it, the two are tied.
- Tampering (Deming) lives in kaizen only, committed.
- Shewhart stays out of the mandate: it has an operator watching.
