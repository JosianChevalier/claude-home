# Harness

**Thesis.** The harness serves one socio-technical system, the human at the centre, augmented by agents. It does not control that system; it harnesses it: its constraints are shaped so the whole tends toward the purpose.

## One system

Three parts are designed: the human's cognition, read as 4E, embodied, embedded, enacted, extended ([[philosophy/collaborative-production]]); the agent's context, the scarce resource ([[philosophy/context-economics]]); and their interaction, collaborative production, the transmission belt. They are not three components but three properties of one system, deeply intertwined, and never designed apart. Every practice caters to all three, and the practices reinforce each other across them ([[philosophy/agentic-extreme-programming]]).

## Friction-feel belongs to the whole

Friction-feel, the engineer's sense of where the model resists, is a property of the whole system: an emergent constraint (Juarrero), set by the mesh, carried by no single part. No part is made responsible for the signal; the whole is designed so that it arrives.

## Three images

Each image shows one aspect of the system:

- **Harness**, as on a horse (Axelrod and Cohen, *Harnessing Complexity*): where the engineer embeds with the agent. A complex system takes no commands, yet it can be harnessed: its constraints are shaped so it tends toward the purpose.
- **Mecha suit**: the agent extends the engineer's intention and amplifies it, rather than acting as an autonomous robot. Like power steering, it removes the effort, and the road stays felt.
- **Transmission belt**: the interaction seen as a transmission. The engineer's type is carried into the code's concept; the medium's load is absorbed, the domain's is transmitted, friction included ([[philosophy/transmission-belt]]).

The strong-style driver is where the three meet: steered, it is harnessed; carrying the intention faster than the engineer's hands, it is a mecha suit; turning the type into code and the friction back, it is a belt ([[philosophy/collaborative-production]]).
