# Writing

**Thesis.** A text is shaped for its reader's cognition: for a human, drawn before it is told, the claim first, short enough to be finished; for an agent, only what departs from its weights. An agent writing from an input mirrors its structure, patchwriting, so the ideas are cut loose from the input before anything is written.

```
 reader   reads with                        so the text
 ──────── ───────────────────────────────── ──────────────────────────────────────
 human    eyes and the body's spatial       is drawn first, prose for the rest
            machinery                       puts the claim before its argument
          attention, stopped at the first   is redone shorter when it overflows
            mistake
 agent    weights, then context             says only what departs from the weights
          the nearest basin: the input      is written from notes, never the input
```

## The figure is the answer

Abstract thought runs on the body's spatial machinery ([[philosophy/embodied-navigation]]). A figure is navigated with it: the eye goes to the box that matters, compares two columns, follows an arrow. Prose is decoded in one line, in the writer's order. So for a human reader the figure carries the answer, and prose carries what a drawing cannot: an argument, a cause, a verdict, a choice.

The form follows the shape of the information: parallel items, a comparison across dimensions, a flow, a loop, a few homes for many items. Each element earns its place by what it saves the reader, as ink earns its place in Tufte's data-ink ratio.

A form is recognised by its conventional mark, the header rule of a table, the bullets of a list, the arrows of a flow; without it, it is read as another form. A figure also carries the metaphor of what it draws, as image schemas do (Lakoff & Johnson): boxes nested say containment, an arrow says causation or time. Drawn from the word instead of the concept, it asserts a model nobody holds.

## The claim first

The governing thought comes before its support (Minto, the pyramid principle). A human reader decides at the top and dives only if the top is in doubt.

It matters more in conversation: the user stops reading at the first mistake and corrects it, because the rest of the message is usually built on it. Whatever follows a wrong first point is written for nobody. So a turn opens with what decides, and holds one decision.

## The reread

"tl;dr", "wall of text", the same point asked again: the message overflowed the reader's attention. The load was extraneous (Sweller), the form's, not the domain's ([[philosophy/zoom-levels]]). Explaining further adds to it; the answer is redone shorter and drawn.

## The agent reader

An agent reads with its weights before its context. What the weights hold costs tokens to restate and steers nothing; what departs from the default is the whole content ([[philosophy/field-terms]]). A precise term steers hardest per token ([[philosophy/attractors]]).

A table or a figure returns less to an agent than to a human: the spatial gain is the eye's, and the relations a layout implies are left to the model's inference, where a sentence states them.

## Patchwriting

**Patchwriting** (Howard, composition studies): the source's structure kept, its words swapped, the text lightly rewritten.

An agent does it by default. The input is the nearest basin in its context ([[philosophy/attractors]]): its order, its sections and its phrasing pull the output, whether the input is a pasted text, a file under review or the user's spoken words. It shows three ways:

- the text comes back in the input's order, reworded
- a list comes back a list, where its items belonged each in its own place
- a frame found in the input, a file's premise or a work file's open question, is taken as the question, untested

The counter is the one composition teaches: notes first, then the text written with the source closed. Every idea is extracted, tagged by where it goes (stays, merges, moves, cut) and reorganized; the new text is written from the reorganization. Cut loose from the input's shape, the ideas stop pulling toward it.

Below a paragraph the input's shape is rarely wrong, and the method costs more than it saves.
