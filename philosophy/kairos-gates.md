# Rationale: Kairos Gates

*Kairos* is the opportune moment, as against *chronos*, mere duration. Because the agentic loop is a built process, it is a place where gates can be added — moments where the model or the practice is reflected on before the belt moves again ([[philosophy/transmission-belt]]).

## Where the library becomes legitimate

The agent holds sedimented culture, not a situated type ([[philosophy/type-and-concept]]). That familiarity is illegitimate as adjudication and legitimate as *supply*. Gopnik's finding on ambiguous figures: reversal is impossible until someone hands you the alternative representation — informed versus uninformed is the variable, not intelligence. A gate that asks whether the DDD conception holds, whether the right question was asked, whether an Order in this codebase is also a revenue event elsewhere — that is the library making a flip available.

The first gate is the request itself. Calibration — the first response, before anything is built — is where a rival reading of what was asked goes on the table: the cheapest moment for a flip, since nothing has been predicated yet. Later gates are adjustments along the way; the first-turn stop in the planning and study skills is this gate, named.

Only the situated type has the horizon to decide whether the flip applies. So: the agent puts a rival figure on the table because it has met it elsewhere; the resolution is the engineer's, because origination requires a horizon the agent does not have. This is sparring in the maieutic sense, and it lives on this axis only — it never crosses into the base layer, where the belt simply runs ([[philosophy/transmission-belt]]).

## The belt feeling its own strain

The agent's own load signals ("I am doing many things at once") belong to this axis: the belt feeling its own strain and reporting it, so that the engineer can decide whether the strain is the task's or the model's.

## Dimension, not value

Transparency and kairos are a distinction of dimension, not of value. Transparency is the belt running; kairos gates are the counsel a smart belt can offer while it runs — upstream in ideation, or as adjustments along the way.
