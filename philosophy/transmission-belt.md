# The transmission belt

**Thesis.** Remove the medium's load, transmit the domain's, friction included.

Seen as a transmission, the harness carries the engineer's type into the code's concept ([[philosophy/model-phenomenology]]). This document says what kind of transmission, and what the lower layers aim for.

```
type ─── intention ───▶ belt ───▶ concept in the code
 ▲                                          │
 └── observe: disappointment ◀── friction ◀─┘
     orient: tension ─▶ destruction ─▶ Stiftung
```

## Belt, not gear

A belt differs from a gear in one property: **compliance**. A gear couples fully: everything the load meets reaches the engine. A belt couples partially: it transmits the work and gives on the rest.

Read as a design principle, the belt gives on the medium's hitches and transmits the domain's tensions, as friction the engineer feels. In variety-absorption terms ([[philosophy/metis-kairos]]): the levels below absorb what they can; what they cannot reaches the engineer through the belt, and that arrival is the kairos.

What arrives is a tool becoming visible. A tool in use disappears into the action, and reappears as an object only when it gets in the way: Heidegger's shift from ready-to-hand to present-at-hand. The domain model is the tool here. Its friction is the signal that it is no longer the right one, and the belt exists so that the signal reaches the hand holding it.

Two failure directions, the belt's, both misclassifications:

- **Too tight.** The medium comes up as if it were the domain: a decision of no consequence for the design, which the engineer gains nothing from knowing. Full noise, cognition overloads.
- **Too loose.** The domain is absorbed as if it were the medium: a friction that belonged in the engineer's Observe is improvised past, a redesign made silently below. The agent sporks, the veteran at scale ([[philosophy/model-phenomenology]]), and reports success. The engineer's Observe starves.

What to pull up and what to absorb depends on the setting, so the philosophy does not draw the line: the doctrine and the know-how of each layer give the belt the heuristics for its own ([[philosophy/three-layer-harness]]). The general test lives in [[philosophy/collaborative-production]].

## The return path

A belt runs both ways. The concept's **Enttäuschung**, Husserl's disappointment, the anticipation that the thing fails to fulfil, has to travel back up to the type, or the transmission has no return path. Without it you get the veteran's condition mechanised: a type that revises while the code does not.

Sweller's three loads are the constraint:

```
extraneous   the medium's   remove     simplify the trivial
intrinsic    the domain's   transmit   make the friction felt
germane      the type's     protect    where the type grows
```

Germane load is the effort that builds the schema: the type grows through disappointment against a concrete concept. A transmission frictionless in that direction too stops the type from growing, and the agent becomes the only thing in the mesh that changes.

## What the return path is for: destruction

The loop is human. Observe, orient, decide, act (Boyd): improving needs a decision; the decision needs an orientation; orienting is destroying an internal world and rebuilding it; and no world gets destroyed without an observation that disappoints it. The harness does not run this loop; it enables it, at every level of the work. Disappointment is the Observe. Orient is the active half, Boyd's *Destruction and Creation*: a closed conceptual system's mismatch with reality grows on its own (Gödel, Heisenberg, the second law), so destruction must be continuous and deliberate. Destructive deduction shatters the existing domain; creative induction reassembles the parts into a new concept. Orient is the capacity to destroy a mental world.

Merleau-Ponty's deviation from maximal grip is pre-reflective: felt before it is thought. Boyd makes it deliberate, a skill: the expert destroys their own gestalt before entropy forces the issue. In Husserl's terms, the epoché turned on oneself: suspending belief not in the world but in one's own grip on it, as a prelude to Boyd's rebuilding.

The full dynamic, the return path of the figure above:

- **tension**: felt loss of Prägnanz
- **destruction**: Boyd, or voluntary disappointment
- **Stiftung**: the founding act after which the new figure is seen and the old one becomes hard to imagine. "This is a *Transfer*", and six scattered fields snap into an object. Model Mitosis is a Stiftung event.

The friction coming up the belt is the Observe step. Orient cannot happen without it: what comes back up is what the engineer destroys and refounds with. An agent that absorbs the disappointment removes the observation, and with it the engineer's Orient.

## The harness, not the agent

The harness is not the agent's intelligence. It is the entire development process, and friction-feel is a property of that process: an emergent constraint in Juarrero's sense, set by the mesh, carried by no single part. Two axes of design follow:

- **Base layer: transparency, for the unknown unknowns.** That tensions will arise is known; their kind, their place, their source are not. The work stays observable to the engineer through feedback on every change, so the unexpected shows and the tension is felt: the channel through which metis perceives. Remove the showing and you have the recruit's condition at the speed of an agent. The form of the feedback changes with the medium; the need does not. How to show: [[philosophy/zoom-levels]]; going to see: [[philosophy/conversation-as-interface]].

  For now, the feedback is built around the code: highlighted diffs, the implications of a change shown as it is made. The engineer moves in abstraction, never out of the code: code written well, at the right levels of abstraction, is the best representation of behavior. A schema carries structure and makes behavior harder to read, so it supplements the code and never replaces it.

## What the lower layers aim for

The properties the lower layers of the harness, rules and skills, should aim for:

- **The conversation is where the belt runs.** The type is predicated into a concept in the exchange; the artifact records the current state of that predication ([[philosophy/collaborative-production]]). The engineer's intention is carried, not improved: a better idea from the library is a rival reading offered at a gate, never a substitution made in the code.
- **Translation is located, not diffused.** Diffusion, the spork ([[philosophy/model-phenomenology]]), is the one failure the belt image predicts most directly: the agent's fluency makes it cheap to produce and invisible to the producer.
- **When the type is silent, the choice returns up the belt.** If the engineer has not chosen among rival concepts, the choice is not made below.
- **Load comes up through several signals.** A large, multi-concern change, a model tension, a heuristic from the technical layers: each comes up as visible implication rather than accomplished fact.
- **Everything shown is shown so that metis can work on it.** Cartography ([[philosophy/zoom-levels]]), attention budget ([[philosophy/context-economics]]) and attractors ([[philosophy/attractors]]) govern how; the belt governs why.
