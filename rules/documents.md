# Documents

Whatever outlives the turn, code included. Terse, plain, short.

## Where it goes

State goes in the shortest-lived store that outlives it: the conversation a turn, the notebook the session, the work file the work, the repo what the work leaves behind. Uncommitted is still conversation.

- The notebook holds the session, a backlog, not a secret diary: steps left, concerns, parked ideas. `grab-notebook` prints its path. Nothing outside the session names it.
- What crosses between sessions belongs to the `ongoing-work` and `handover` skills.

## Bureaucracy

LLMs carry an additive bias (Adams): they add beside, and keep. It never lets up. A document holds the ruling, not the minutes of the trial.

- Rewrite, never amend: the reader sees the result, never the steps, as from outside a refactoring (referential transparency). « now », « instead of », « no longer » are amendments: cut them.
- How a decision was reached is load for nothing: the reader wants the hole, not the drill (Levitt). What is dropped is deleted, never prohibited: kept, even as a ban, it gets closed questions reopened, sabotage by the book (OSS, 1944). The reason and what lost go in the commit message.
- An open decision keeps its options: they are its state.
- Remove scaffolding so it does not outlive its purpose: debug traces, temporary files, a done work file. Don't let it turn into a sidewalk shed. What comes next may need one line, where it is needed.

## Patchwriting

An LLM's output takes the structure of its input: the source's order, sections and overlaps survive, only the words change (patchwriting, Howard). This bias is fought actively. The file being rewritten is a source too.

- Prewriting, as taught at school: the ideas dumped in the notebook, in your own words, then mapped. The document is drafted from the map, the source closed.

## Writer-based prose

Writer-based prose (Flower) follows the order the writer met things. A document tells no story: the syuzhet is chosen for the reader, the result first.

- Walk the document as its reader, cold: at each line, what do they need next? A line that costs more than it carries goes: one to read past, a term never defined, a pointer to open.
- For a human: a figure of the whole on top, headings that tell the file alone, the text under them for the agent. A document walked in steps can carry a map on top and a you-are-here at each step.
- For an agent, the field's own term: "bounded context", not "module". A behavior the model goes to unprompted is named by its pejorative ("primitive obsession").
