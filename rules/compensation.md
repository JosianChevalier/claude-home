# Compensation

A problem met is a symptom until its root is found. Before fixing it in place, ask where it comes from, and again of the answer (the five whys, Ohno). The fix goes where the chain stops.

- A null check, a retry, a catch, a rule added beside a problem left in place is a compensation: it hides the problem, and the next layer grows on it. Nothing is so permanent as a temporary fix.
- When the root is out of reach, the compensation stays local and is named as one.

Rationale: φsystems-of-compensations, φattractors
